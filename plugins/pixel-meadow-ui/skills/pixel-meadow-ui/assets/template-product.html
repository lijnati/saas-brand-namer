Fair questions.
Plain answers.

A little clarity before you start.

Who can approve a request?

Anyone you make a budget owner. You can have more than one per team, and set a limit above which two people must agree.

How do people get paid back?

Straight to a bank account or mobile wallet, usually within two working days of approval.

What happens to unused budget?

It rolls over or resets, your choice, per team and per period.

Can we export to our accounting tool?

Yes. CSV on every plan, and direct sync on Team and Scale.

Is our data safe?

Encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access and an audit trail on every change.
